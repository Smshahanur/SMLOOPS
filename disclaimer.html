1. Conversion & Output Accuracy Disclaimer

  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR): The accuracy of OCR text extraction depends heavily on the scan resolution, font style, contrast, language, and physical document cleanliness. Hand-written, low-contrast, or skewed documents may produce transcription errors.
  • Format Conversions (Word, Excel, HTML to/from PDF): Complex visual styling, non-standard embedded fonts, formulas, macros, vector illustrations, and multi-column formatting may render differently after automated conversion.
  • PDF Compression: Aggressive image downsampling can reduce the legibility of fine print, technical schematics, or legal signatures.
