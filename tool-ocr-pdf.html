What is OCR & How Does SMLOOPS Process It?

When paper documents are scanned, traditional scanners generate a photographic snapshot of the page (known as a raster bitmap) rather than true digital typography. The result is a PDF that looks like text to a human, but to a computer is merely millions of coloured pixel dots. You cannot highlight, copy, search, or paste from it.

Optical Character Recognition (OCR) bridges this divide by examining pixel patterns and reconstructing digital characters.

SMLOOPS provides complete client-side privacy by executing Tesseract.js in browser Web Workers:

  • Page Image Extraction: Each PDF page is rasterized at high resolution (1.5x scale) onto an HTML5 canvas in memory.
  • Binarization & Contrast Analysis: The OCR worker analyzes edge contours, baseline alignments, and character geometry.
  • Neural Pattern Matching: Tesseract matches pixel arrangements against trained language typography datasets.
  • Formatted Text Generation: The recognized characters, line breaks, and page divisions are assembled into an easily readable text document.

Because processing runs inside local Web Workers on your device, sensitive financial records, legal transcripts, or personal letters are never exposed to remote third-party cloud servers.

Factors Affecting OCR Accuracy

Tips to Maximize Text Quality:

  • Scan Resolution: Scans at 300 DPI produce substantially higher accuracy than low-res 72 DPI scans.
  • Document Alignment: Severe page skew (crooked scanning) can confuse line baselines. Straight scans provide cleaner word separations.
  • Contrast & Background: High black-on-white contrast yields near-perfect accuracy. Faded receipts, dark yellow paper, or stained invoices may require visual proofreading.
  • Handwriting vs Print: The engine is trained on printed typography. While neat block handwriting may transcribe decently, cursive script is not reliably supported.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my document uploaded anywhere during OCR?

No! The entire Tesseract OCR neural model runs locally in your web browser via WebAssembly. Your files never touch external servers.

How many pages can I OCR at once?

There are no artificial limits. Processing time scales with page count and device CPU speed (typically 1–2 seconds per page).

Can I copy the text directly into Word or Google Docs?

Yes. The resulting .txt file can be opened in any text editor, copied to your clipboard, and pasted directly into Word, Docs, or Excel.
