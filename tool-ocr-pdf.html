OCR PDF Online

Extract readable text from scanned PDF pages. This page explains the workflow and gives practical guidance before you use the tool.

Open OCR PDF

What OCR means

Optical character recognition, or OCR, attempts to recognize characters in an image or scanned page and turn them into machine-readable text.

How to use OCR PDF

Select a scanned PDF and start the OCR process. The recognition stage can take longer for documents with many pages or detailed images.

What affects accuracy

Clear scans generally produce better results. Blur, skew, handwriting, unusual fonts, low contrast, tables, columns, and complex layouts can reduce recognition accuracy.

Always review extracted text

OCR output should be checked before it is used for important records. Names, numbers, dates, and formatting can be recognized incorrectly even when most of a page looks correct.

Important: Keep an original copy of important documents. After processing, open the output and verify that the pages, text, images, and formatting are correct before sharing or submitting it.
