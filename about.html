About SMLOOPS

SMLOOPS (Smart Tools in One Loop) is an independent web utility platform built to solve everyday digital file challenges directly in the web browser. We empower students, educators, small business owners, remote professionals, and developers to manipulate PDFs, extract text, and convert digital media without sacrificing confidentiality or paying expensive recurring software subscriptions.

The SMLOOPS Philosophy: Client-Side Privacy First

When the internet was young, simple tasks like merging two PDF pages or compressing an oversized resume required downloading heavyweight desktop programs or uploading sensitive personal documents to unknown remote servers. Traditional cloud converters upload your tax returns, bank statements, identification scans, and proprietary contracts across the internet, where they sit on third-party servers waiting to be processed.

SMLOOPS is built on a fundamentally different principle: 100% Client-Side In-Browser Processing.

By taking advantage of modern browser capabilities—including the HTML5 File API, WebAssembly (Wasm), and advanced client-side JavaScript engines—our tools read and manipulate file bytes entirely within your computer’s or smartphone's volatile memory. Your documents never travel across an external API, never touch our servers, and are destroyed from local memory the moment you refresh or close your browser tab.

🔒

Our Technology Stack & Open Standards

We believe in architectural transparency. SMLOOPS leverages audited, high-performance open-source libraries that power modern digital standards:

  • PDF-Lib: Enables in-browser creation, page manipulation, text embedding, rotation, and cryptographic restructuring without native binaries.
  • PDF.js (by Mozilla): The industry standard for parsing, rendering, and rasterizing PDF viewports directly onto HTML5 canvases.
  • Tesseract.js: Pure JavaScript port of the renowned Tesseract OCR engine, running optical character recognition in browser web workers.
  • SheetJS (xlsx): Client-side spreadsheet workbook parsing and tabular data extraction for Excel conversions.
  • Mammoth & Docx: Document Object Model conversion between Microsoft Word DOCX formats and standard HTML/PDF outputs.
  • JSZip: Client-side archive bundling for batch document exports and image extraction.

Who SMLOOPS Serves

Our tools are designed for diverse real-world workflows:

  • Academic Students & Researchers: Merging research papers, citations, and syllabi; compressing large slide decks to fit university LMS submission portals (Canvas, Blackboard, Google Classroom).
  • Job Applicants & Freelancers: Combining resumes, cover letters, and reference letters into polished single-file portfolios; signing contracts digitally without printing.
  • Small Businesses & Accountants: Extracting transaction tables from bank statements to Excel, watermarking confidential draft proposals, and organizing scanned expense receipts.
  • Legal & Administrative Teams: Applying sequential Bates numbering to discovery files, removing blank separator sheets from scan batches, and organizing document exhibits.
