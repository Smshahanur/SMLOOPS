Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Welcome to SMLOOPS (accessible at https://smloops.vercel.app/). We believe that digital file tools should be private, secure, and respectful of your data. This Privacy Policy transparently outlines how information is handled when you interact with our website, use our utilities, and view advertisements.

Zero Server-Side Document Uploads

Unlike cloud conversion platforms, SMLOOPS executes file processing client-side in your web browser. Your PDF files, scanned images, documents, and spreadsheets never leave your device, are never transmitted across external servers, and are never saved to any database.

1. What Data We Process

A. Your Documents and Files

When you select a document for merging, compressing, splitting, editing, or optical character recognition (OCR), the file is loaded directly into your browser's local sandbox memory (using HTML5 File and ArrayBuffer APIs). All byte calculations and format manipulations are performed on your local CPU. Once processing completes, the converted file is saved directly to your local downloads folder. Closing or refreshing your browser tab clears volatile memory entirely.

B. Communications & Inbound Inquiries

If you choose to contact us via our contact form or directly via email (shahanurmollah1@gmail.com), we collect the name, email address, and message contents you provide solely to reply to your inquiry, provide technical support, or resolve reported bugs. We never sell, rent, or trade your contact information.

C. Standard Technical Logs

Like virtually all web hosts, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically logs basic technical request metadata, such as IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, referring URLs, and timestamps. This information is used exclusively for network diagnosis, DDoS mitigation, and ensuring server uptime.

2. Google AdSense & Advertising Disclosures

SMLOOPS is financed in part through digital advertising to keep our tools freely accessible to the public without paywalls. We work with Google AdSense and third-party advertising networks.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to SMLOOPS and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info or Your Online Choices.

3. Cookies and Web Storage

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to enhance your browsing experience. SMLOOPS uses cookies for:

  • User Interface Preferences: Remembering light/dark mode settings and category display preferences stored in your browser's localStorage.
  • Advertising & Analytics: Enabling Google AdSense to serve relevant advertisements, prevent ad fraud, and cap advertising frequency.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective websites.

4. Third-Party CDNs & Open-Source Libraries

To ensure rapid page loading and robust cryptographic operations, SMLOOPS loads open-source libraries (such as PDF-Lib, PDF.js, Tesseract.js, Mammoth, and SheetJS) via trusted global Content Delivery Networks (CDNs), including Cloudflare (cdnjs), jsDelivr, and unpkg. These CDNs receive standard network requests for script files, governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance)

Depending on your geographical location, you have statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You have the right to request copies of any personal correspondence we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification & Erasure: You may request correction or deletion of your communication records.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing: SMLOOPS does NOT sell or share personal information with data brokers.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of young children is especially important. SMLOOPS does not knowingly collect any Personally Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child provided personal information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect technical enhancements or regulatory changes. The revised date at the top of this page will always indicate when modifications were enacted.
